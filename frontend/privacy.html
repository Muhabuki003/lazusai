Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LazusAI ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when businesses ("Clients") use our AI receptionist service, and when individuals ("Customers") message a business that uses our service. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and in accordance with applicable law, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) where it applies.

1. Information We Collect

  • Client account information: business name, contact details, billing information, and dashboard credentials.
  • Business configuration: services, pricing, hours, staff names and phone numbers you provide so the AI can book correctly.
  • Customer conversations: text messages sent to a business number using the Service, including sender phone numbers, message content, and appointment details.
  • Booking records: dates, times, services, names, and any notes captured during booking.
  • Usage data: pages visited, feature usage, and diagnostic logs needed to operate and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the AI receptionist: respond to messages, check availability, and create bookings.
  • To notify staff and owners of new bookings and escalations.
  • To bill clients and provide support.
  • To maintain security, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.
  • To improve accuracy and reliability of the Service. We do not sell personal information.

3. Sharing of Information

We share information only: (a) with the business operating the number you messaged, (b) with service providers who help us run the Service (hosting, messaging infrastructure, payment processors) under confidentiality obligations, (c) when required by law or legal process, or (d) with your consent. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention

Conversation and booking data is retained while your business account is active and for a reasonable period afterward (up to 30 days post-termination) unless a longer retention is required by law (e.g., medical or tax record obligations, for which the business remains responsible).

5. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit, restricted access controls, and secure infrastructure, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA & others)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to: know what personal information we collect; request deletion; correct inaccurate information; opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" (we do neither); and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. To exercise these rights, contact us at founder@bookistudios.com. We will verify requests and respond within 45 days.

7. Sensitive Information

Businesses using the Service for healthcare appointments must comply with HIPAA and confirm they have appropriate safeguards and patient consent for the information processed. Customers should not send information they are not comfortable sharing over text.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service evolves. Material changes will be posted here with an updated revision date. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: founder@bookistudios.com
